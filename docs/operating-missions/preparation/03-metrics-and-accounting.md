All example targets, schedules, allocations, horizons and rounding rules are **fixture-only**, not approved business policy.

This package gives Codex explicit expected results for the accounting and measurement contracts in [Part II §§10–17 and §21](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9). ## 1. Current source and the required distinction

The existing revenue-outcomes report deliberately selects a completed job's positive `amountPaid`, otherwise its positive `invoiceTotal`, otherwise `estimateTotal`. It reports a materials-only contribution basis and describes period activity rather than validated cohorts. Its material calculation uses job product quantities and catalog costs. These are inspected legacy behaviors, not newly reproduced defects and not proof that the OM earned-revenue or historical-cost contract already exists. The new OM definitions must preserve compatibility while using separately named, source-backed projections. [Current report](https://github.com/SimonShaun2/Renvani/blob/7840884d346ca95788e2668cc78148afa3b6a843/apps/api/src/services/revenue-outcomes.service.ts) · [Current report types](https://github.com/SimonShaun2/Renvani/blob/7840884d346ca95788e2668cc78148afa3b6a843/packages/types/src/revenue-outcomes.types.ts)

The specification requires distinct facts for accepted work, verified performed service, finalized invoice allocations, settled cash, agreement recognition and historical variable costs. A payment acknowledgment, approved estimate, completed review or generic `agent.execution_succeeded` event cannot supply missing facts. Legacy queued receipts must remain distinguishable from provider acceptance and verified delivery. [Part II §§17.1–17.2 and OM-A15/16/30/33](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9) · [Current receipt source](https://github.com/SimonShaun2/Renvani/blob/7840884d346ca95788e2668cc78148afa3b6a843/apps/api/src/services/agent-action-receipt.ts)

## 2. Shared units, identity and completeness

### Money and rates

Persist money as decimal strings with an explicit currency. The fixture currency is `USD`; input amounts have exactly two decimal places, such as `"1000.00"`. Arithmetic uses `Decimal`, not binary floating point. Credits and refunds have positive magnitudes and explicit adjustment kinds; computed differences can be signed. The fixture display rule is `ROUND_HALF_UP` to two decimals after division. Production allocation, rounding, supported currencies and FX policy remain **OM-D03**; this file supplies no cross-currency aggregation or implied USD default for a tenant.

Count metrics use nonnegative integers. Rates retain their exact integer numerator and denominator; `1 / 3` is not stored as an exact `0.33`. Money per job/opportunity is denominated in the selected currency per canonical unit. Delays use elapsed **seconds**, computed from UTC instants; UI days/hours are formatting, not an alternate denominator. The fixtures use whole-second instants and do not establish production subsecond precision.

### Time and revisions

Every normalized fixture event includes `eventId`, `canonicalFactId`, `companyId`, `sourceEntity.type/id`, positive `sourceVersion`, `occurredAt`, `receivedAt`, `kind` and `data`. IDs are readable synthetic references, not payloads to a real endpoint. `sourceVersion` is a local example revision, not a claim that every current table exposes an ordered version column. A server-trusted tenant and genuine source lineage must be established by implementation. [Part II §§4, 17.2 and 19](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9)

Store occurrence and receipt instants in UTC; preserve both. Attribute performed-service revenue to the verified service period in the business's declared IANA timezone, not the invoice issue date, ingestion date or machine timezone. Attribute cash to its settlement/refund period. Freeze timezone and period interpretation with the report/cohort policy version. Current `companies.timezone` is an available source, but its schema default does not verify a particular business's configuration. [Current schema](https://github.com/SimonShaun2/Renvani/blob/7840884d346ca95788e2668cc78148afa3b6a843/packages/db/src/schema.ts)

For a calendar-month report, the intended period is the half-open interval from the first local midnight to the next local month's first midnight, converted using that declared timezone. Local calendar days are not assumed to be exactly 24 elapsed hours around clock changes. Example **OM-FIN-04** places `2026-11-01T04:30:00Z` at `2026-10-31T23:30:00-05:00` in `America/Chicago`. Its November invoice revises October earned service; its November payment remains November cash.

One canonical result contributes one current source revision to each relevant metric. Preserve superseded versions, source adjustments and correction lineage. Exact event replay or a new transport receipt for the same source revision adds no value. Same-identity/same-version conflicting content requires reconciliation, not summation. The fixture oracle rejects that conflict; it does not implement a durable reconciliation mechanism. A soft-deleted source must retain its accounting/audit history. [Part II §17.2](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9)

### Unknown is different from zero

An explicit zero is valid only for the stated complete scope or a known numerator. Missing source coverage, unsupported recognition, missing historical cost or unresolved linkage is unavailable/partial and must carry a reason. In **OM-FIN-05**, recognized revenue is zero and the unbilled completed-job count is one; the amount pending billing is unknown and is never replaced by an estimate. In **OM-FIN-08**, the included agreement's earned component and complete earned total are `null`, while its known earned subtotal is `"0.00"` and cash is `"1200.00"`.

The closed synthetic examples establish which facts exist for their declared view. The oracle cannot infer completeness of a live source from the absence of an event. Required missing thresholds, cost components, maturity rules and authority never default to passing. [Part II §§11, 17 and OM-A34](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9)

## 5. Adjustment, cohort and outcome rules

For a gross eligible service allocation, earned revenue is the allocation less each linked canonical revenue credit once. For an already-net allocation, embedded credit references remain provenance and are not subtracted again. A linked cash refund changes cash, not earned revenue a second time. A refunded unearned deposit changes its own cash allocation without producing negative earned service. One result can have several mission contributors, but those edges carry no duplicate economic amount. [Part II §17.2; OM-A14/16/33](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9)

Freeze the eligible service-need cohort and rule/version before treatment, or define a prospective enrollment stream in advance. Retain eligible people who were not selected, did not respond, declined or did not convert. Different legitimate needs for one customer can be distinct opportunities; a reschedule cannot create a new one. Real ambiguous identity correction remains **OM-D07**. Acquisition can change who becomes an inquiry, so revenue per observed inquiry/opportunity alone does not identify causal acquisition effect. [Part II §17.3](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9)

Keep three independent dimensions: **work performed**, **verified observed business outcome**, and **estimated causal effect**. A completed workflow may have a `pending`, `target_not_met`, `not_evaluable` or `target_met` outcome verdict. A numeric target with a failed required guardrail is not a mission win; required missing evidence does not pass. Even an observed target-met result has no attributable effect claim without the declared valid design, maturity and uncertainty evidence. Pilot baseline/sample/prior thresholds remain proposed and unevaluated under **OM-D11**; this package builds no estimator. [Part II §§6, 17.1, 17.4–17.5 and OM-A34](https://app.notion.com/p/40e317b92d1581188031e8a0a4c847c9)

## 7. Local consistency check and its limits

From the repository root, run:

```bash
python docs/operating-missions/preparation/validation/validate_accounting.py
```

The observed local result for this artifact version is:

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Scenarios and declared checkpoints | 19 scenarios; all 43 checkpoints matched |
| Supplemental period checks | 2 passed: exact local timestamp/month and alternate November cash query |
| Selected final-state reorder checks | 57 passed: reverse, canonical fact/version sort and one-position rotation for each case |
| Final-state replay checks | 38 passed: exact replay and same source facts with new transport IDs for each case |
| Negative fixture mutations | 8 rejected as expected |
| Production behavior / runtime acceptance | **Not tested / not executed** |

The eight negative mutations check double subtraction of a linked refund from earned revenue; failure to subtract a refund from cash; float money; foreign tenant contamination; conflicting same-fact/same-version content; immediate agreement recognition without policy; dropping a frozen nonconverter; and presenting a partial-cost remainder as complete contribution.

The oracle checks the expected keys present in each checkpoint and rejects unknown expected keys. It uses simple local selection of the newest canonical source revision. The three named reorderings are **not exhaustive permutations**. Replay checks compare final calculated fixture state; they do not demonstrate database idempotency, transaction safety, durable processing receipts, RLS, outbox delivery or crash recovery.

Its deliberate limits are material:

- All monetary examples use scale-2 USD and whole-second times. It does not validate FX, currency-specific precision, tax calculation, fee allocation, fractional-cent allocation, financial statements or a real accounting basis.
- All evaluated financial checkpoints have their referenced source facts present. It does not implement orphan buffering or resolve ambiguous/conflicting real sources. A credit-before-refund overpayment/credit balance is outside its nonnegative-open-receivable examples.
- The payment-duration case has no credits, reversals or reopened invoices. It verifies the stated three-invoice timing arithmetic, not a production censoring/competing-risk estimator or every payment endpoint policy. Its overdue/undisputed/positive-balance candidates do not check the due-plus-one-business-day calendar rule or any authority/contact gate and are not send-eligible results.
- Agreement milestones and required cost components are explicitly chosen fixture inputs. The checker does not approve them, verify a real obligation, establish historical cost coverage or supply customer/billing authority.
- Cohort amounts/classifications and operational evidence are synthetic normalized inputs. It does not prove the real extraction, eligibility, identity merge, receipt verification, contribution allocation or detector implementation.
- It verifies selected expectations, not an exhaustive schema for all future events. It imports no application module and contacts no database, provider, model or network. No CI wiring or production type export is added.

Passing this script means the preparation examples are internally consistent. Codex still needs meaningful application tests against real domain services, deterministic event/revision logic, authorization/tenant boundaries, source corrections and accepted UI/API behavior before any associated OM acceptance can pass.
